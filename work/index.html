Work

Everything below is live and public. The client work that pays for it mostly is not, and will stay that way.

  • AnalyticsZone

    Founder and developer

    A family of sports-analytics apps for running, swimming, cycling, walking, sleep and general health. They read what your watch already recorded and compute the models coaches actually use — critical power and W′ balance, critical swim speed, gait asymmetry, HRV-based recovery.

    Everything is computed on the device. No account, no upload, no server-side copy of your health data. Each metric page cites the paper the model comes from.

  • onmedic

    Founder, since 2000

    The health-technology studio behind the rest. Clinical data systems and research software for hospitals, research centres and laboratories, most of it confidential.

  • Pelvisana

    Founder

    Pelvic-floor education and training, written with clinicians and reviewed by them, in Catalan, Spanish and English.

  • Velytra

    Founder

    A multisport coaching platform connecting coaches with the athletes they train.

  • DoctorMi

    Founder

    Secure, non-urgent clinical communication — the boring, necessary layer between a patient and their doctor.

The apps

Each one covers a sport and shares the same metric engine. They read from Apple Health or Health Connect, compute locally, and require no account.

  • Run Analytics — VO₂max, training zones, load
  • Swim Analytics — stroke efficiency, CSS zones
  • Bike Analytics — FTP, critical power, W′ balance
  • Walk Analytics — gait asymmetry, mobility
  • Health Analytics — the Apple Health metric library
  • Sleep Analytics — sleep staging, HRV, recovery
  • Paceva — all of the above, in one app
